# Academic integrity

This website supports learning and professional documentation. It must not undermine university rules or academic honesty.

## Principles

1. **Personal reflections only by default**  
   Course pages contain personal learning notes and interpretations. They are not official teaching materials.

2. **No restricted course materials**  
   Do not reproduce slides, assignment briefs, exam content, or other restricted university materials.

3. **No unauthorised solutions**  
   Assignment solutions are not published unless the university or course rules explicitly allow publication, or the work is original practice outside restricted assessment.

4. **Verify official information**  
   Programme details, syllabi, schedules, and requirements should be confirmed through official Luleå University of Technology sources. This site may lag behind or summarise incompletely.

5. **Publication-safe code only**  
   Published code should be personal practice work, clearly public examples, or material explicitly approved for sharing.

6. **Honest progress claims**  
   Do not present incomplete coursework, unfinished projects, or unverified results as completed achievements.

7. **No endorsement implication**  
   This is a personal portfolio and is not an official website of Luleå University of Technology.

## Recommended page wording

Use statements such as:

> This page contains personal learning notes and does not reproduce restricted course materials.

## Before publishing course-related content

- Confirm the material is not restricted
- Prefer your own explanations and summaries
- Link to official sources when publicly available
- Keep assessment integrity intact for yourself and others
