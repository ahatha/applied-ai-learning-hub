Degree composition

  • 75 ECTS compulsory coursework
  • 15 ECTS electives (normally two 7.5 ECTS courses)
  • 30 ECTS master thesis
  • 120 ECTS degree total
  • Optional Swedish course (S0046P, 3 ECTS) is outside the degree

High-level purpose

Year 1 (confirmed schedule): programming for machine learning, AI and pattern recognition, knowledge/reasoning, business context, neural networks, data science programming, advanced deep learning, and AI in healthcare.

Year 2 (official planned schedule): advanced data mining, applied AI, elective choices, and the master thesis. Elective listings are options, not a personal enrolment list.
