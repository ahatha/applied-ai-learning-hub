Why this hub exists

I bring around 17 years of IT experience, including more than a decade in solution architecture across Java systems, web services, enterprise integrations, SQL, and cloud platforms. I am now formally studying applied artificial intelligence while building practical skills in Python, data analysis, and machine learning.

This transition is an extension of systems thinking, not a sudden restart. Architecture experience helps with problem framing, integration design, and responsible delivery — capabilities that remain valuable when working with data and models.

The site will evolve throughout the 2026–2028 programme. Status labels are intentional: they distinguish existing strengths, skills being learned, work in progress, and planned future study.
