Distance study

This is not an on-demand or self-paced programme. Study normally takes place during daytime working hours, and scheduled activities may occur approximately between 08:00 and 17:00. Active participation is expected. Most lectures are delivered live, and recordings may be available only in limited cases.

Arrangements can vary by course. Always verify current expectations through official LTU course information.
